Add `ApplicationCommand` and its managers, fetch-only like `WebhookManager`: `client.application.commands` (`ApplicationCommandManager`) and `guild.commands` (`GuildApplicationCommandManager`, a new instance on every access, also `client.guilds.commands(guildId)`) have `fetch`, `create`, `set`, `edit`, `delete`, `commandPath` and the static `transformCommand`, which accepts camel-cased data, raw payloads and builders. `ApplicationCommand` has the command's fields, `edit()`, the `set*()` helpers, `delete()`, `permissions`, `equals(command, enforceOptionOrder?)` (`false` for a foreign argument) and the static `optionsEqual()` and `transformOption()`. `ApplicationCommandPermissionsManager` (`fetch`, `has`, and `set`, `add` and `remove` taking an explicit bearer `token`, with no OAuth handling) reads through the existing `applicationCommandPermissions` cache, kept up to date by `APPLICATION_COMMAND_PERMISSIONS_UPDATE`. `GatewayError` gains the `ApplicationCommandPermissionsTokenMissing` and `GlobalCommandPermissions` codes. `set()` is the bulk overwrite and replaces whatever `@wolfstar/http-framework` registered, nothing calls it for you.
